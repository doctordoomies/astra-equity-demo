Astra Equity — Investment in gaming ventures

Capital for the ventures players build.

Astra Equity funds and supports promising gaming ventures, from Minecraft servers and gaming networks to esports projects and online communities.

01Thesis

Gaming ventures are built by their communities. We fund the people who build them well.

Many lasting businesses in gaming begin small: a server, a network, a team, a community that keeps coming back. They are run by operators who know their players better than any outside observer could.

Astra Equity exists to back those operators with capital and steady support, and to stay with them over the long term.

02Focus

Where we invest.

  • 01

    Minecraft servers

    Independent servers and server networks with an established player base.

  • 02

    Gaming networks

    Multi-title networks and the infrastructure that keeps them running.

  • 03

    Esports projects

    Teams, leagues and events with a clear plan for sustainable operation.

  • 04

    Online communities

    Communities and platforms where players gather, create and stay.

  • 05

    Digital businesses

    Tools, services and studios that serve players and the people who run games.

03Approach

Selective by design, committed for the long term.

We make a small number of investments and give each one real attention. That means saying no often, and meaning it when we say yes.

Capital is where the relationship starts, not where it ends.

  • Selectivity

    We would rather back a few ventures well than many ventures thinly.

  • Long-term commitment

    We invest with patience and plan to stay through the difficult stretches.

  • Hands-on support

    We work alongside founders on the problems in front of them.

04Process

From first conversation to decision.

  1. 01

    Submit

    Tell us about your venture, your community and what you are building toward.

  2. 02

    Review

    We review what you send and follow up where there may be a fit.

  3. 03

    Conversation

    If there is a fit, we talk in detail about the venture and how we could work together.
